Tru Form 360 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

The short version

  • You create a free account with Sign in with Apple. Apple lets you hide your email from us if you like.
  • Your journal is saved on your iPhone and backed up to your own private account, so you don't lose it if you change phones. Only you can read your backup.
  • We never sell your information, and there is no advertising and no tracking.
  • Packaged foods you add from a label (with their barcode) are shared with other members, without your name, to make the food database more accurate for everyone.
  • You can export your data, or delete your account and everything in it, at any time from inside the app.

Who we are

Tru Form 360 ("we", "us") makes the Tru Form 360 app, a tool for tracking food, water, fasting, weight, body measurements and healthy habits. Tru Form 360 is operated by Tru Form 360 LLC, a Texas limited liability company. You can reach us at truform360@gmail.com.

Your account

To use the app you sign in with Sign in with Apple. When you do, Apple gives us:

  • a private account number that identifies you to our app;
  • your name, if you choose to share it (we use it only to greet you and in your account details);
  • an email address: either your real email or, if you pick "Hide My Email", a private relay address from Apple that forwards to you. We use it only to contact you about your account or this policy, never for marketing without asking.

We never see your Apple ID password. You can sign out at any time in More > Account & Backup. Signing out backs up your journal and then removes it from that phone; signing in again brings it back.

What the app stores

The information you enter is saved on your iPhone and in your private cloud backup:

  • Food entries: names, calories, protein, carbs, fat, and the date and time you logged them
  • Foods and recipes you create yourself in My Foods, and the amounts you last used for foods
  • Water intake, and whether you prefer ounces or millilitres
  • Fasting start and end times
  • Weigh-ins, and optional waist and body-fat measurements (including weigh-ins copied from Apple Health, if you connect it; see below)
  • Your daily targets and goals
  • Your setup answers: sex, birth year (worked out from the age you enter), height, how active you are, and whether you want to lose, keep or gain weight. These are used only on your iPhone to suggest your daily calorie and protein targets, and are not sent to the AI coach or anyone else. You can change them with "Run Setup Again" in More.
  • Your habit tracker: which habits you marked "Had it" or "Avoided" each day (for example soda or sweets), the healthy habits you checked off (for example exercise, sleep, prayer or meditation), and any habits you add yourself, with the names you give them
  • Your faith choice, if you make one (see "Faith choice" below)
  • Your daily reading plan progress, if you start one
  • Your reminder settings
  • Your AI coach settings (coach voice and name), the goals you set with your coach and their progress, and your latest weekly pep talk

A few things stay only on your iPhone and are not backed up: the record that you accepted the welcome screen, your Apple Health connection setting, and a second safety copy of your journal used to recover it if the main copy is ever damaged.

Cloud backup

A few seconds after each change, and when you close the app, the app sends a copy of the information listed above to your private backup. This lets you restore your journal on a new or reset iPhone by signing in.

  • Where it is kept: with our hosting provider, Supabase, on servers in Canada (Montreal region). Supabase stores it for us under a data processing agreement and may not use it for anything else.
  • How it is protected: it travels over an encrypted connection and is encrypted when stored. Our database only lets a signed-in person read or change their own backup; other users cannot see it.
  • Who at Tru Form 360 can see it: we do not look at your journal. Our team can technically access the database to run and fix the service, and will do so only when needed to keep the app working, when you ask us to (for example to help with a problem), or when the law requires it.

AI coach (optional)

The AI coach is powered by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, PBC. The first time you open it, the app explains this and asks for your permission. If you agree:

  • What is sent: your messages to the coach, any photo you choose to send, the short list of things the coach remembers about you, and a summary of your information from the app so the coach can help you: food totals and food names, your saved My Foods, targets, water, fasting, weigh-ins, the habits you track and marked, your coaching goals and progress, your reminder settings, and your faith choice if you made one.
  • Who receives it: our server passes it to Anthropic to get the coach's reply. Anthropic processes it for us under its commercial terms and does not use it to train its AI models. Anthropic may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring, as its policies describe.
  • What is kept: we don't store your coach conversations on our servers. We keep a daily count of coach messages to enforce the daily limit. The few lasting facts the coach remembers about you (for example your goals or foods you like) are saved on your iPhone and in your private backup. You can see and erase them any time with the coach settings button (person icon) in the coach, and "Delete all my data" or "Delete my account" erases them.
  • Greeting when you open the app: unless you turn it off in the coach settings, your coach says hello when you open the app (at most every few hours). To write that greeting, the same summary is sent to Anthropic.
  • Changes the coach makes: when you ask, the coach can log foods, mark habits, start or end a fast, set targets, goals and reminders for you. Each change is shown in the chat with an Undo button.
  • Microphone: if you tap the microphone, your iPhone's speech recognition (provided by Apple) turns your voice into text.
  • Coach voice: if you turn on the speaker, the text of the coach's reply (not your messages, photos or health numbers) is sent from our server to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech to create the spoken audio. Google processes it for us as a service provider and does not use it to train its models. The audio is played on your phone and not kept on our servers.
  • AI can make mistakes. The coach's food numbers are estimates, so check them and use Undo or Edit if they're off. Nothing it says is medical advice.

Suggestions and problem reports (optional)

If you use "Suggest a Change" in the More tab, we save your message, the part of the app you chose, the app version and your iPhone model (for example "iOS 26.1") to your account so we can review it and post a reply you can see in the app. We use AI tools to group similar requests and help us decide what to build or fix first. Please don't include health or personal details in your message. Your suggestions are deleted when you delete your account.

Food search

When you search for a food, only the words you type are sent: through our server to FatSecret (the FatSecret Platform API), and over the internet to the U.S. Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central service, to find matching foods and their nutrition values. FatSecret sees only our server, not you. Your IP address is visible to the USDA as with any internet request. We do not send your name, your journal, your weight or any other data. Each service's own policies apply.

Barcode scanning

If you scan a barcode, the app asks for permission to use your camera. The camera is used only to read the barcode number; no pictures or video are recorded or saved. The barcode number is sent through our server to FatSecret, and over the internet to the Open Food Facts database and if needed the USDA's FoodData Central service, to look up the product's nutrition information. Your IP address is visible to Open Food Facts and the USDA as with any internet request; FatSecret sees only our server. No other information is sent. Each service's own policies apply.

On the Scan tab, the app gives the product a score from 1 to 10. The score is worked out on your phone from the product information the database returns: 55% nutrition, 30% ingredient concerns such as additives, and 15% how processed the food is. There is no bonus for organic or brand claims.

If you add or correct a product with "Add it from the label" or "Fix it from the label", the food is saved in your My Foods and your backup, and it is also shared with other members as described in the next section.

Shared food database

Tru Form 360 members build a shared food database together. When you save a packaged food from its Nutrition Facts label together with its barcode, we share these details with all members: the barcode, the food's name, the serving size, and the calories, protein, carbs and fat on the label. Your name, email and account are never shown to anyone, and nothing from your food journal (what you ate, when, or how much) is shared.

  • Behind the scenes we record which account added, confirmed, used or reported a shared food, only so we can count it once per person, mark a food "member-verified" when a second member enters the same numbers, stop misuse, and show you how many people your foods have helped. Other members never see this.
  • Home recipes and foods you create without a barcode stay private to you.
  • If you report a shared food's numbers as wrong, that report is counted; foods reported by several members are hidden.
  • If you delete your account, your records of adding, confirming, using or reporting foods are deleted. The shared food entries you added stay in the database for other members, with no link to you.

Faith choice (optional)

When you first open the app, it asks whether you practice a faith (Christian, Jewish, Muslim, Buddhist, Hindu, Spiritual, or None / prefer not to say). Answering is optional; you can skip it. Your choice is used only inside the app to pick your daily encouragement, the verse on the opening screen, the name of your reading habit and which daily reading plan is offered.

Religious belief is sensitive information, so we want to be clear: your choice is saved on your iPhone and in your private backup, so it comes back if you change phones. It is never shared with anyone and never used for advertising. You can change it any time in More > Faith & Encouragement; "Delete all my data" and "Delete my account" erase it.

Daily reading plans (optional)

If you start a reading plan (Bible, Tanakh, Quran, Dhammapada or Bhagavad Gita in a Year), the app saves your start date and which readings you marked as read. Tapping "Read" opens the day's reading on a separate website: Bible Gateway (New International Reader's Version), Sefaria, Quran.com, Access to Insight or Holy-Bhagavad-Gita.org. Only the address of that reading is opened; no information from the app is sent to these sites. Each website has its own privacy policy.

Reminders (optional)

If you turn on a reminder in More > Reminders, your iPhone asks whether Tru Form 360 may send you notifications. Reminders are scheduled and delivered by your iPhone itself. Your reminder settings are part of your backup, but no reminder is sent from our servers.

Apple Health (optional)

If you tap "Connect Apple Health", your iPhone asks whether to let Tru Form 360 read your weight, steps and active calories. You choose what to allow and can change it any time in the iPhone's Settings app under Health > Data Access & Devices > Tru Form 360, or turn off sync inside the app.

  • The app only reads this information. It never writes or changes anything in Apple Health.
  • Steps and active calories are shown on the Today screen and are not backed up.
  • Weights read from Health are copied into your weigh-in history in the app, so they are included in your private backup. They are used only for your progress chart and your daily water goal.
  • We do not use Apple Health information for advertising or marketing, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with data brokers or other third parties. We never store it in iCloud.

Tru Form Plus (optional subscription)

Tru Form Plus is an optional paid subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Apple takes the payment; we never see your card or payment details.

  • When you subscribe or tap Restore Purchases, the app sends Apple's transaction number for your purchase to our server. Our server asks Apple to confirm it and then saves: which plan you have, whether it was a test purchase, its status (for example active, expired or refunded), when the current period ends and when you first subscribed. This is linked to your account so Plus works on any iPhone you sign in on, and so one subscription can't be used by a different account.
  • Apple also tells our server directly when a subscription renews, is cancelled or is refunded, so your Plus status stays correct.
  • If you join with the January 2027 Founding price, we save your Founding Member badge and, for the first 50 members, your badge number.
  • If you are given a family code and use it, we save that your account used it.
  • To cancel, go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone. Deleting your Tru Form 360 account deletes this information from our server, but it does not cancel your subscription with Apple, so please cancel it in Settings as well.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect your location or contacts. The camera is used only while you scan a barcode or take a meal photo you choose to send to your coach; the microphone is used only while you talk to your coach, and Apple turns your words into text. We don't keep the photos or any recordings.

Information handled by Apple

Apple provides the App Store, TestFlight and Sign in with Apple, and has its own privacy practices. Apple may collect information such as crash reports, usage statistics and, if you choose to send it, beta feedback. Please read Apple's privacy policy. Your iPhone may also include the app's data in your iCloud or computer backups, depending on your own device settings.

Exporting your data

"Export my data" in More > Your data creates a copy of your information and opens the iPhone share sheet. What you do with that copy is up to you. Please be careful, because it contains personal health information.

Deleting your data and your account

  • Delete all my data (More > Your data) erases your journal from your iPhone and from your backup, but keeps your account so you can start fresh.
  • Delete my account (More > Account & Backup) permanently deletes your account, your backup and the data on your iPhone. This happens right away and cannot be undone. Copies in our hosting provider's routine backups, if any, are removed automatically within 30 days.
  • Deleting the app from your iPhone removes the data on that phone but not your account or backup. Sign in again to restore it, or delete your account from inside the app first.
  • You can also email truform360@gmail.com to ask us to delete your account or to tell you what information we hold about you.

How long we keep it

We keep your backup for as long as you have an account. If you delete your account, we delete your backup as described above. We do not keep separate copies of your journal.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example Washington, California or other U.S. states with health data or privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your information, and to withdraw your consent. You can do most of this inside the app; for anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

If we ever learn that your health information was accessed without permission, we will tell you as the law requires.

Not medical advice

Tru Form 360 is a tracking and wellness tool. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition and is not a substitute for a doctor or dietitian. Talk to a healthcare professional before making major changes to your eating, fasting or exercise.

Children

The app is for people 18 and older. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

If this changes

When we add new features that handle your information differently, such as a paid subscription or the community, we will update this policy before they are released and ask for your clear permission where the law or Apple requires it. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
