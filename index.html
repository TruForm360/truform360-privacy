Tru Form 360 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026 (beta)

The short version

  • Tru Form 360 keeps your information only on your own iPhone.
  • There are no accounts, and the app does not send your health information to us or to anyone else.
  • There is no advertising and no tracking in this version.
  • You can export or delete everything at any time from inside the app.

Who we are

Tru Form 360 ("we", "us") makes the Tru Form 360 app, a tool for tracking food, water, fasting, weight, body measurements and healthy habits. You can reach us at truform360@gmail.com.

What the app stores

When you use the app, the information you enter is saved on your device only:

  • Food entries: names, calories, protein, carbs, fat, and the date and time you logged them
  • Foods and recipes you create yourself in My Foods
  • Water intake
  • Fasting start and end times
  • Weigh-ins, and optional waist and body-fat measurements. If you connect Apple Health, weigh-ins read from Health are copied into this history on your phone.
  • Your daily targets and goals
  • Whether you turned on Apple Health sync
  • Your habit tracker: which habits you marked "Had it" or "Avoided" each day (for example soda or sweets), and which healthy habits you checked off (for example exercise, sleep, prayer or meditation, and any habits you add yourself, with the names you give them)
  • Your faith choice, if you make one (see "Faith choice" below)
  • Your daily reading plan progress, if you start one: the start date and which readings you marked as read
  • Your reminder settings: which reminders are on and the times you picked
  • A record that you accepted the welcome screen
  • A second safety copy of the same information, also on your phone only, so your journal can be recovered if the main copy is ever damaged

We do not receive any of this. It never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

Food search

When you search for a food, the words you type are sent over the internet to the U.S. Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central service to find matching foods and their nutrition values. Your IP address is visible to that service as with any internet request. We do not send your name, your food journal, your weight or any other data, and we do not receive the searches ourselves. The USDA's own policies apply to its service.

Barcode scanning

If you tap "Scan barcode" in Add Food, or use the Scan tab, the app asks for permission to use your camera. The camera is used only to read the barcode number. No pictures or video are recorded or saved. The barcode number is sent over the internet to the Open Food Facts database to look up the product's nutrition information. If Open Food Facts does not have the product, the same barcode number is sent to the USDA's FoodData Central service instead. Your IP address is visible to these services as with any internet request. No other information is sent. Each service's own policies apply.

On the Scan tab, the app also gives the product a score from 1 to 10 (Approved, Slightly off or Not approved). The score is worked out on your phone from the product information the database returns: 55% nutrition, 30% ingredient concerns such as additives, and 15% how processed the food is. There is no bonus for organic or brand claims. Scanned products are not saved unless you choose to add one to your food journal or save it in My Foods.

Faith choice (optional)

When you first open the app, it asks whether you practice a faith (Christian, Jewish, Muslim, Buddhist, Hindu, Spiritual, or None / prefer not to say). Answering is optional; you can skip it. Your choice is used only inside the app to pick your daily encouragement, the verse on the opening screen, the name of your reading habit and which daily reading plan is offered.

Religious belief is sensitive information, so we want to be clear: your choice is saved only on your iPhone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising. You can change it at any time in More > Faith & Encouragement, and "Delete all my data" erases it.

Daily reading plans (optional)

If you start a reading plan (Bible, Tanakh, Quran, Dhammapada or Bhagavad Gita in a Year), the app saves your start date and which readings you marked as read, on your phone only. Tapping "Read" opens the day's reading on a separate website in a browser window: Bible Gateway (New Living Translation), Sefaria, Quran.com, Access to Insight or Holy-Bhagavad-Gita.org. Only the address of that reading is opened; no information from the app is sent to these sites. Each website has its own privacy policy, which applies while you use it.

Reminders (optional)

If you turn on a reminder in More > Reminders (for example to log a meal, drink water, weigh in, or the evening encouragement), your iPhone asks whether Tru Form 360 may send you notifications. Reminders are scheduled and delivered by your iPhone itself. Nothing is sent to us or over the internet. You can turn any reminder off in the app, or turn off notifications for Tru Form 360 in the iPhone Settings app under Notifications.

Apple Health (optional)

If you tap "Connect Apple Health", your iPhone asks whether to let Tru Form 360 read three kinds of information from the Health app: your weight, your steps and your active calories. You choose what to allow, and you can change or turn off that access at any time in the iPhone's Settings app under Health > Data Access & Devices > Tru Form 360, or turn off sync inside the app.

  • The app only reads this information. It never writes or changes anything in Apple Health.
  • The information stays on your iPhone. We do not receive it, and the app does not send it to us or to anyone else.
  • We do not use Apple Health information for advertising or marketing, and we do not sell it or share it with data brokers or other third parties.
  • Steps and active calories are shown on the Today screen. Weights from Health are copied into your weigh-in history in the app and are used for your progress chart and your daily water goal. Deleting your data in the app removes those copies. It does not delete anything in Apple Health.

What we do not do

  • We do not create accounts or ask for your name, email address or phone number inside the app.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information. The only data sent anywhere is the food search text and barcode numbers described above.
  • Notifications are used only for reminders you turn on yourself.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or microphone data. Apple Health is read only if you connect it. The camera is used only while you scan a barcode, and nothing it sees is saved.

Information handled by Apple

If you install the beta through TestFlight, or later through the App Store, Apple provides that service and has its own privacy practices. Apple may collect information such as crash reports, usage statistics and, if you choose to send it, beta feedback and screenshots. Please read Apple's privacy policy. Your iPhone may also include the app's data in your iCloud or computer backups, depending on your own device settings.

Exporting and sharing your data

The "Export my data" button in More > Your data creates a copy of your information (including your habit tracker, faith choice, reading plan progress and reminder settings) and opens the iPhone share sheet. What you do with that copy, and who you send it to, is up to you. Please be careful, because it contains personal health information.

Deleting your data

You can erase all your information from the app at any time with "Delete all my data" in More > Your data. This erases your journal, the safety copy, your habit tracker, faith choice, reading plan progress and reminder settings, and cancels any scheduled reminders. Deleting the app from your iPhone also removes its stored data. Because we do not have a copy, we cannot recover deleted data for you.

Not medical advice

Tru Form 360 is a tracking and wellness tool. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition and is not a substitute for a doctor or dietitian. Talk to a healthcare professional before making major changes to your eating, fasting or exercise.

Children

The app is for people 18 and older. It is not directed to children.

If this changes

Future versions may add features such as accounts, backup across devices, blood-test uploads or an AI coach. Those features would handle your information differently. Before we release any of them, we will update this policy and ask for your clear permission where the law or Apple requires it. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
