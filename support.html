Signing in and your backup

Tru Form 360 uses Sign in with Apple. Your journal is saved on your iPhone and backed up to your private account. If you get a new phone, install the app and sign in with the same Apple ID, and your data comes back.

To back up right away, go to More > Account & Backup > Back up now.

Exporting or deleting your data

  • Export: More > Your data > Export my data.
  • Delete your account: More > Account & Backup > Delete my account. This removes your account and all of your backed-up data. It can't be undone.

Logging food

Tap + Add Food under any meal, or the round green button at the bottom of the screen. Search by name, scan a barcode, or pick from your history. If a packaged food's numbers don't match the label, choose "Fix it from the label". Your fix helps everyone who scans that food.

The AI coach

Tell your coach what you ate in plain words and it will log it for you. You can undo anything it adds. The coach gives general wellness encouragement only. It can make mistakes, so check its numbers against your food labels.

Apple Health

Tru Form 360 can read your weight, steps and active calories from Apple Health, only if you allow it. It never writes to Apple Health. To change this, open the iPhone Settings app > Health > Data Access & Devices > Tru Form 360.

Reminders

Turn reminders on or off in More > Reminders. If none arrive, check that notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings > Notifications > Tru Form 360.

Health note

Tru Form 360 is for general wellness and is not medical advice. Talk with your doctor before starting a new diet, fasting or exercise plan, especially if you are pregnant, have diabetes or another medical condition, or take medication.
